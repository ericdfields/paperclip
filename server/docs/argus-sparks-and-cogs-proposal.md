Proposal (quick)
----------------
1. Use "sparks and cogs" as the lead metaphor in an early paragraph of the thesis post to give readers an immediate human frame. Short, punchy example: "At Argus, users are the sparks — curators, storytellers and judgment-makers. Argus provides the cogs — the cataloging, rights tracking and moderation plumbing that accelerate creative work."
2. Add one short sub-section titled "Sparks and Cogs" that maps existing platform capabilities to the metaphor (catalog = cogs; moderation = cogs; discovery = cogs; user curation & composition = sparks).
3. Finish with a call-to-action that reframes product decisions: "We build the paved road so sparks can create; we don't replace the spark." This reinforces 'sell the road, not the engine.'

Suggested placements and edits (if doing a light edit pass)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Lead paragraph (top 1/3): prepend a one-sentence metaphor to orient non-technical readers.

  Suggested insert (1-2 sentences):

  "Think of creative work as sparks and cogs. Sparks are the human acts of judgment — curation, composition, rights and ethical choices. Cogs are the reliable, mechanical systems: cataloging, indexing, moderation, and metadata plumbing. Argus is the infrastructure of cogs that lets people focus on the sparks."

- Mid-post architecture section: add a small table or bullet list mapping product features to 'cogs' and user-facing outcomes to 'sparks'. This helps engineers and PMs see alignment.

- Closing paragraph: strengthen the thesis with a direct sentence tying to the original line 'paved road frees humans for judgment work', reformulated with metaphor: "We sell the paved road of cogs so humans can do the sparks only they can do."

Companion short landing copy (for marketing / hero section)
------------------------------------------------------
Headline: "Argus: Where Sparks Create and Cogs Deliver"
Subhead: "Give your team the mechanical certainty of modern media systems — cataloging, rights, moderation and discovery — so humans can focus on creative judgment and storytelling."
Bullets (3):
- Cogs that run: cataloging, rights, moderation, searchable metadata
- Controls that matter: curation, composition, ethical review
- Faster loops from idea to publish: less manual plumbing, more judgment

One-liner for product page: "Argus builds the invisible machinery so your people can be the spark."
